🔒 Privacy Policy

No data leaves your device. PayBuddy stores everything — your shifts, salary, tax settings, and payroll data — exclusively in your browser's local storage. Nothing is transmitted to any server, database, or third party.

No account required. PayBuddy does not collect your name, email address, employee number, or any personally identifiable information.

No tracking or analytics. PayBuddy does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any analytics tools.

Your right to erasure. You can delete all stored data at any time by clearing your browser's local storage, or by using your browser's "Clear site data" function. All data is immediately and permanently removed.

GDPR compliance. As PayBuddy processes no personal data on any server and stores data solely on your own device, it operates outside the scope of data controller obligations under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). You retain full control of your data at all times.

By continuing you acknowledge that you have read this policy.
